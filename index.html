RevoHuman

High-fidelity human demonstrations for contact-grounded dexterous manipulation.

01 / OVERVIEW

Robots need more than video.
They need grounded interaction.

Contact-rich manipulation depends on continuous hand configuration, wrist motion and physical contact. A single egocentric camera cannot reliably recover all three.

RevoHuman combines human task capability with direct multimodal measurement and a portable data pipeline, targeting the three-way trade-off between task capability, data fidelity and scalable collection.

02 / SYSTEM

Capture the full interaction,
not a proxy for it.

RevoHuman directly measures the motion and contact variables that matter for dexterous manipulation, then aligns them with human-centered visual context.
